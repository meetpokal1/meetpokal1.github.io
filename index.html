BUSINESS FINDING

Revenue is not the same as profit

Basmati Rice 5kg leads product revenue at about ₹1.65 Cr, yet its modeled gross margin is only 2.15% (about ₹3.54L gross profit).

SCENARIO ANALYSIS

Stress-test a pricing decision

Under the stated model assumptions, a 3% price adjustment can tolerate up to a 58.3% unit-volume decline before gross profit falls below baseline. This is a scenario, not a forecast.

OPERATIONAL ACTION

Prioritize stock exposure

40 stock positions are truly out of stock, 96 are below reorder, and the replenishment heuristic suggests 5,791 units at an estimated ₹9.66L cost.

Problem-Solving Approach: Frame → Audit → Engineer → Investigate → Deliver

Audit the Data

Deliver & Validate

Produce decision-ready outputs and test key calculations so users can understand where the results came from.
