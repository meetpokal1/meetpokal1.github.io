Flagship Analytics & Data Engineering Platform

InsightFlow: Retail Profit & Data Integrity Intelligence

CI verified · 233 tests · Release cleanup merged

Business Problem: Retail and wholesale teams often receive inconsistent CSV and Excel exports with duplicate customer profiles, malformed values, invalid relationships, and stock records that are difficult to reconcile. That makes margins harder to trust, replenishment harder to prioritize, and data changes harder to explain. InsightFlow builds a repeatable Python cleaning and validation pipeline, models the cleaned data in PostgreSQL, exposes tested SQL and API analytics, and presents decision evidence through a four-page Power BI report plus a locally runnable Streamlit dashboard.

₹20.90 Cr Audited Net Revenue (7,878 Orders)
₹4.24 Cr Gross Profit (20.3% Margin)

Pipeline Architecture & Data Quality: Built a 3-step automated data quality pipeline (scripts/run_pipeline.py) that ingests 8 disparate tables, detects missing values and anomalies, and executes deterministic cleaning rules. The pipeline successfully validated 8,734 clean orders (rejecting 212 invalid records with documented rejection reasons), repaired 230 sign-flipped negative quantities, merged 40 duplicate customer profiles, and loaded clean star schema views into PostgreSQL.

Analytical Findings & Business Value: The unfiltered multi-year dataset reveals a 20.3% overall gross margin across ₹20.90 Cr net revenue. Inventory intelligence identifies critical stock imbalances—highlighting SKUs requiring priority reorder versus overstocked items binding liquidity. The RFM customer segmentation engine isolates the top revenue-contributing accounts, enabling targeted account management and churn risk mitigation.

Business Intelligence & Governance Insight:

Replacing unversioned Excel reports with an automated, test-backed pipeline eliminates manual cross-tab errors and guarantees reproducible reporting. Business leaders review clean, single-source-of-truth KPIs while analysts retain full auditability through the automated data quality rejection log.

Analytical Approach & Competencies

Data Integrity Over Vanity Dashboards

Dashboards are only as dependable as the transformation pipeline beneath them. My work prioritizes writing reproducible Python parsing logic, testing mathematical edge cases, and catching discrepancies before records surface in executive summaries.

Relational Modeling & Normalization

Structuring transactional data into clean 3NF relational schemas prevents redundant records and integrity errors. I focus on writing clear SQL queries, establishing unambiguous primary and foreign keys, and designing clean star schema marts.
